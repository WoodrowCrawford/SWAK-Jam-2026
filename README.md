There's a legend that, deep down into the open maw of the forest, your wishes will be granted by one of the fair folk. What is freely given may only be borrowed, however, lest the fae come collect more than you bargained for... And to those who belong to the forest and to the forest must return, fate will take them back. 

One way or another.
Whether they wished for it or not.

Description
Pas de Deux: A Duet of Roses  is a story-driven game where the choices you make change the outcome of the story. Join us for a dark fairytale set in the Alps, with creatures inspired from its folklore and many more enchanting creatures.

What will you be able to uncover?
Can you find the secrets hidden among the trees of our enchanted forest?
